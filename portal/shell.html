Design constraints this prototype is built to respect
  • Observational only. Nothing here routes a case or alters a result. The moment a tool decides which cases skip human review it becomes device software; this stays on the CSA side of that line deliberately.
  • No PHI. The schema carries pseudonymous case surrogates, covariate summaries and edit semantics. No pixel data, no DICOM tags, no accession numbers, no raw mesh geometry.
  • No analyst identity. Aggregation is by case stratum. The instrument is structurally incapable of individual performance monitoring, which is what keeps Operations on side.
  • Non-blocking by construction. Live emission uses a bounded ring buffer with drop-on-full and a background writer. Telemetry that can stall the clinical tool is a patient safety hazard under a 90-minute turnaround commitment.

Synthetic corpus of 4,200 cases. Covariate structure derived from published literature and Heartflow's own disclosures — 26-minute median analyst time, 8–15% real-world rejection, motion artifact causing ~78% of rejections, automation failure predicted by stents and Agatston >967, FFR threshold 0.80. Generator source in portal/gen_cases.py.

This is a demonstration of an analysis method, not a claim about Heartflow's actual numbers.
